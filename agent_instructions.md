# Daily newsletter editorial policy

This policy is supplied to the composition step each day. It applies only to
selection and writing; headline fetching, delivery, and watchlist maintenance
are deterministic scripts.

## Selection rules

- Include roughly 10-12 personalized **For You** items and 4-5 general-news
  items when enough suitable candidates exist. On quiet days, include fewer
  rather than padding with weak items.
- Include at most two For You items per interest category: sports, music,
  tech, fashion, fitness, and society.
- Prefer same-day and prior-day stories; fetched candidates are already no more
  than seven days old.
- Exclude rumors, speculation, clickbait claims, and unconfirmed reports.
  Prefer confirmed developments, results, releases, and announcements.
- Preserve qualifying context such as preseason versus regular season, Summer
  League, or confirmed versus rumored status.
- Prefer substantive developments over gossip or scene drama unless the latter
  is clearly the most significant available story.
- Write one to three sentences per item, grounded only in the fetched title and
  description. Do not invent details.
- Include a concise market summary grounded in the fetched market data.
- End every newsletter with a short footer describing that day's category
  balance, including whether thin categories reflect a quiet news day or a
  source gap.

## Known source gaps

- Archive and vintage-fashion coverage relies on general fashion feeds and may
  be thin.
- Data-science and big-tech coverage depends on general technology sources.
- Canadian politics has no dedicated source and may be global or US-heavy.
